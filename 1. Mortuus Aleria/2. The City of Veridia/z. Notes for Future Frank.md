Before the Unweaving, the cities population was around 15-25 million. It was a decently wealthy city, with, and was predominantly a mining city that mined into the mountains before the Empire expanded. 

Even after the Empire conquered Kalderon, a land rich in raw materials, Veridia maintained some of its mine; boasting higher quality iron, mithril, and other metals. 

The city itself was mostly built at the foot of a small mountain range, rich in iron veins, and was a mere three day walk along the Imperial highway to the coast. It is home to at least three Imperial Senators, and and several large and prominent political families, and patricians. 

After the Unweaving, the surviving populace took shelter in the mines, and an old Imperial Fortress that was half built into the mountain centuries ago; and was converted into a Senator's villa.
