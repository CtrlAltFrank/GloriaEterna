Priests, zealots, mystics, and the desperate commoners who cling to the belief that the Gods will restore order; and that the destruction of the [[Aleria]], and the silence of the Gods is a test. 

- [[Aelius Caelestis]]
   -  De-facto leader of The Faithful, he leads surmons and has a large following of zealots, and terrified citizens.

- [[Lysandros]]
   -  Closer to a neutral party; he has a following of truthseekers and fortune tellers, and those who wish to know how this will all end.

- [[Sabrina Honoris]]
   -  Perhaps more powerful than [[Aelius Caelestis]] in numbers of loyal zealots, a splinter faction of The Faithful who believe sacrifice will resolve their sin.
