**Name**: Marc Dione
**Race**: Human
**Age**: 42
**Rank/Profession:** Blacksmith turned Relief Laborer
**Faction**: [[4. Civilians|Veridian Civilian]]
**General Appearance:** 
- Short and stocky. Soot smudged arms. He still wears his thick leather apron, now repurposed to hold scavenged tools. His arms show multiple burn scars.
**Personality**:
- Gruff, but kind.
- Grumbles about lost commissions for work, yet will share his last loaf of bread with a stranger. 
- Hardwork redeems most sin.
**Hooks**: 
- Offers to repair or fashion simple weapons.
- Suspicious of strangers, will quiz the players on where they came from.
- Haunted by guilt over causing a large fire during the Unweaving. 
**Backstory**:
- Marc ran a busy forge before the Unweaving, and now goes out with patrols to scavenge for tools. During the Unweaving, he was working on an Imperial commission, and during the initial storm he let the forge grow too hot; causing a large fire that raged for the first week after the Unweaving.
