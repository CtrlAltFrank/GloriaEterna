**Name**: Lysandros
**Race**: High-Elf
**Age**: 38
**Rank/Profession:** Oracle of Tears, and Prophet of Grisenne
**Faction**: [[3. The Faithful|The Faithful]]
**General Appearance:** 
- Ethereal, and almost translucent skin, matching porcelain hair. 
- Teardrop shaped Ruby necklace hanging at their throat. 
**Personality**:
- Mystique cloaked in unpredictability.
- Delivers truth's that wound, and visions that terrify.
**Hooks**: 
- They foresee The Faithful will be betrayed from within.
- He actively blames himself for not forseeing The Unweaving
- Rumor's say that he and [[Lucius Marcellan]] actively conspire together.
**Backstory**:
- Born under an Omen Star (a comet with a trail), Lysandros answered the call of Grisenne as a youth; as she found him crying deep within a forest one day, and raised him as one of her prophets. 
- When the Unweaving fell, his visions grew darker, he wanders the halls of Aurea, followed by zealots.
