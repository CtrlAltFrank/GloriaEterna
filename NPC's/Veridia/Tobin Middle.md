**Name**: Tobin Middle
**Race**: Halfing
**Age**: 18
**Rank/Profession:** Tinker & Trinket Trader 
**Faction**: [[4. Civilians|Veridian Civilian]]
**General Appearance:** 
- Bright eyed, patchy cap. 
- Wears a vest with pockets overflowing with bronze and stone trinkets.
**Personality**:
- Eager and chatty, he'll sell anything he can find worth selling.
- Begs for news of the Outworld. 
- Extremely homesick.
**Hooks**: 
- Offers to rig simple Arcana traps for a few coin, or scraps of food.
- He is missing parts to fix a broken music box.
- Willing to pay with Arcana trinkets for rumors. 
**Backstory**:
- Tobin was an apprentice under a Master Clockmaker in [[Veridia]]'s artisan quarter. When the Unweaving shattered the shop, he became a scavenger.
