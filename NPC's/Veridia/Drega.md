**Name**: Drega
**Race**: Dwarf
**Age**: 16
**Rank/Profession:** Tunnel Worker 
**Faction**: [[4. Civilians|Veridian Civilian]]
**General Appearance:** 
- Stout, with calloused hands. Long black hair braided into a beaded fashion in front of her face.
- Wears scraps of leather sewn into her clothes. 
**Personality**:
- Pragmatic and stubborn.
- Believes every problem can be solved with elbow grease, and enough hands.
**Hooks**: 
- Pleads for help clearing a blocked shaft that holds food stores.
- She hears things rumbling deeper in the mountains.
- Wants to trade carved runestones for assistance mapping the new tunnels.
**Backstory**:
- Drega was born to a mining family, and she escaped the first wave of the Unweaving by sealing herself in a mineshaft. Weeks later she emerged, and made her way towards Veridia, and the safety of [[Domus Aurea]]
