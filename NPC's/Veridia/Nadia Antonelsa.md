**Name**: Nadia Antonelsa
**Race**: Halfling
**Age**: 22
**Rank/Profession:** Ward of Velmara 
**Faction**: [[3. The Faithful|The Faithful]]
**General Appearance:** 
- Petite and spry. Bright green eyes that flicker back and forth.
- Wears a necklace of bleached antlers. Carries several pouches.
**Personality**:
- Empathetic, foolish, and restless. 
**Hooks**: 
- She pleads with the Dead for forgiveness and deliverance. Tormented spirits seem to haunt.
- She claims to have sealed a chapel filled with angry wraiths. 
**Backstory**:
- Born to a small merchant family, she was orphaned in the Unweaving. After reaching safety, she took a vow to shield the living from the dead. She claims Velmara's blessing.
