**Name**: Aelius Caelestis
**Race**: Human
**Age**: 63
**Rank/Profession:** Hierophant of Targelus
**Faction**: [[3. The Faithful|The Faithful]]
**General Appearance:**
- Robed in dark gray bronze trimmed vestments. Where's a gold circlet.
- Tall and gaunt, visible veins on the forehead. 
- Wide, fanatic eyes. 
**Personality**:
- Commanding presence, fused with conviction.
- Demands absolute obedience to the Gods and their plans. 
**Hooks**: 
- He claims he has received visions foretelling that [[Gaius Marcellan|Gaius]] will fall.
- He hoards fragments of Targelus's bronze crown; an ancient relic. 
- He is planning a mass exodus into the waste, towards the sea.
**Backstory**:
- Born to a modest patrician house, Aelius felt the calling of the Gods as a young boy. He led Veridia's Temple of Sovereigns until the Unweaving scattered his block. 
- He led several thousands survivors to the Temple of Targelus at the cities center, but had to abandon it when it was overrun by Shriven.
