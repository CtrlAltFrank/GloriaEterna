### 

The [[Rentz]] rose to prominence nearly a century ago; establishing itself as a family skilled in banking, trade, and the management of debts. From a modest begining in [[Kobellstone]], they expanded their purse strings across the centrals pains and river network; quietly building a financial empire that would rival the influence of less noble houses; and even some Druids. 

Their wealth allowed them to operate in open trade, and subtle market manipulation, making them indispensiveable to more powerful houses; yet their Plebian rise left them with little true legitimacy. 

During the chaos of the Unweaving, and the [[Ferraxian]] Civil War, the [[Rentz]] used their resources and networks to strengthen their position; with cities and towns destabilizied, and noble and patrician families alike left without their massive markets in [[Aleria]], the [[Rentz]] swiftly moved to acquire debts, and secure overland trade routes to keep the regional commerce flowing. 


### A Knife in the Dark

Their outward showing of neutrality was strategic, and allowed them to act as an intermediate between traders from [[House Barbalani]] to the South, and the [[Fiorellia]] to the North-West; all while feeding information from these trade networks to the [[House Kobielli|Avrialian]] spymasters. 

Behind the ledgers and invoices, lies a deep network of information and cyphers. River Captains carry false manifests, and warehouse clerks maintain two sets of accounts. The usage of trusted debtors in key merchant houses and port guilds has allowed the [[Rentz]] to monitor [[Delrath]] troop movements, misroute convoys, and delay shipments.

When outright disruption was needed, the [[Rentz]] would use their network of couriers and spies to fund small deniable raids on border villages, or guide [[House Fontana|Fontain]] river galleys across the Pontic River. 

Even at home, [[Baroness Selene Rentz]] maintains carefully placed agents within Kobielli's bureaucracy; sleeper agents, and couriers who can report on the goings on of the Court, or act in an emergency.

### [[House Kobielli|Kobielli]] and [[Rentz]]

[[House Kobielli]] view the new upstart [[Rentz]] as a famly of wealth and influence, skilled in manipulating debts and trade across Ferrax. The Kobielli see the [[Rentz]] as a useful tool, rather than equals; and their influence is ackknowledged, but always carefully monitored. 

For the Kobielli, the [[Rentz]]'s slow and methodical infiltration into noble bloodlines, and subtle manipulation of the Fontain family is tolerated; so long as it serves the interests of the [[House Kobielli|Avralius]]. As such, [[House Kobielli]] extends power indirectly through the [[Rentz]] financial networks; and the banking family is well aware that if they lose the patronage of [[House Kobielli]], the careful plans they have laid will come crashing down around them.
