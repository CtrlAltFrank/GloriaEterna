# Relations

### Fontana and [[House Kobielli|Kobielli]]

[[House Fontana]] remain a client vassal loyal to Avralius, bound to them by marriage and blood through younger branches of the family; with [[Baro Giotto de Fontain]] married to a cousin of [[Buto Kobielli]], cementing Fontana's loyalty to Avralius's central authority. 

Yet the alliance is not without its strain; as many [[House Kobielli|Kobielli]] see Fontana as their lesser, a house that is too desparate... To mercantile. But their sealing bond is one of utility; and they are too valuable to dismiss, yet too new to ever be seen as an equal. 

House Fontana owes its very existence to the patronage of the [[House Kobielli|Avralius]], and know that they are both Servant and Symbol; a for all to see that the [[House Kobielli|Avralius]] reward merit. And as such; they see [[House Kobielli]] as both Father and Keeper; for they are the patron whose blessing sustains them, yet whose shadow they cannot escape.

# Origins and Rise of The Fontana

House Fontana is new blood amongst the nobility of [[Ferrax]]; barely more than a century and a half old. Their founder, [[Giovanni Fontan]], was once a Master Architect and Engineer who served the Legions of [[Aleria]] create fortifications along the Pontic River, that assisted in keeping the barbarians from [[Fae Barbaria]] from spilling to Ferrax's Heartlands 

In Recognition, Avralius of [[House Kobielli]] elevated Giovanni's family to full nobility, granting them stewardship on the fertile northern bank of the Pontic River.

Though they have been elevated to Nobility for over a century, many of the Old Families do not consider [[House Fontana]] to be true nobility; as their wealth and status was granted from the skilled work of lay-men. 

House Fontana, and the minor families that it has spawned, still seek to prove themselves worthy of the Title that [[House Kobielli]] bestowed upon them. 

# The Ferrax Civil War - Fontana

The Pontus River, the vast artery that cuts Ferrax from South-East to North-West, played a vital part in the Ferrax Civil War, with no other front being as vital. 

No other front of the Ferraxian Civil War was more vital than the Pontus River; for whoever controlled this vast artery, controlled trade, grain, and movement across the continent. 

### The Night Crossing

In the early months of the war, House Barbalani launched a campaign that gained its matriarch, [[Hiltegundis Barbalani]] infamy and admiration alike. 

Under the cover of a moonless night, and river fog, the Barbalani forces, supported by rivermen from [[House Aangrlio]], made a daring river crossing out of the city of [[Skradin]], landing a quarter of a days march from Cattinara; deep inside Fontana held lands.

These pitch painted barges landed undetected; and quickly overwhelmed the small garrison of Cattinara before dawn; and within the coming days, the banners of Barbalani flew over half a dozen towns Fontana towns. 

The assault shocked the Houses of [[House Kobielli|Avralius]]; but [[Baro Giotto de Fontain]] reacted quickly and mobilized the Fontana; yet the speed and ferocity of the southern advance forced the Fontana into a fighting retreat  

The Fotnana counteroffensive, backed by Avralian reinforcements, halted the Barbalani's northern spearhead; at the cost of tens of thousands of peasants being displaced, and dozens of villages burning to the ground in the fighting. 

--- 

### The Battle of Pontium

While the war raged on the north bank of the Pontus River, the city of Pontium became the war's most bitter prize.

Straddling the Pontus River, Pontium (I'm very original) had always been a symbol of unity; a city of bridges, and trade; the city itself laying at the Southern most mouth of the river, and spilling into the seas beyond. 

Avralius forces, supported by shock infantry from the [[Montagni]], laid siege to the northern districts of the city for three long months; with little to no progress. 

In a brazen mid-day assault, a small party of mercenaries set off several large explosive barrels at key points within the city; including two of the three bridges that connected the city; before making their escape on a stolen river galley. 

This assault signaled the beginning of a two pronged attack; as [[House Kobielli|Kobielli]] Griffin Knights landed at near the cities main Forum, and the remaining Bridge; while a joint Fontana-Montagni northern assault quickly overwhelmed the confused city garrison.

Despite their initial success, The Battle of Pontium devolved into a stalemate; with the northern half of the city firmly in Avralius control, and the other firmly held by the Delroth. For the remainder of the Civil War, open warfare in Pontium devolved into bitter river crossings, and small bands committing acts of assassination sabotage. 

---

### Talsberg Ante Bellum

To the North-West, [[Cecco de la Wald]] saw little use in fighting a Civil War; and Talsberg adopted a policy of isolationism, preferring to defend their shared border, while keeping vigil over the the northern oceans for fear that the Jade Empire would seek to take advantage of the destruction of [[Aleria]]. 

It was near Olus Erba and Talium that a small group of mercenaries ambushed a Fontana trade caravan; slaughtering the caravan master and his guard, and freeing the cargo, before burning the wagons. 

When confronted by their employer near Krisa, these mercenaries convinced the local garrison that the woodsmen of House Talio ambushed the caravan; and later with some deception and forged dispatches, persuaded several Fontana nobles that Talio had entered an alliance with the Delroth.

In the second year of the [[Ferraxian]] Civil War, Avralius pushed northward towards Talium, seeking to prevent a pincer attack by a join Talsberg-Delroth alliance.

The fighting was fierce, but brief; with both sides bleeding heavily for little to no gain; and by winter, and the careful politicking of a small party that acted as mediators, the front stabilized; ending the Talsberg War in an uneasy status quo, with the borders unchanged, but thousands of new graves dug. 



# Gavarian Occupation


Laying at the center of a massive island that split the Pontic River, Gavaria had long served as the grain vault and trade nexus of [[Ferrax]]. And when the Ferraxian Civil War first broke out, and the collapse of the central [[Assembly of Houses]], Gavaria's Signoria declared its neutrality. 

Yet Baro Giotto de Fontain saw an opportunity in the chaos; and set his eyes on the Emerald jewel of Gavaria; and despite the hesitation of Giotto's advisors, he began to mobilize the entirety of the Fontana river flotilla in secret. 

A small band of hired Galivanti, were able to make their way into Gavaria, crossing the mighty bridge that connected it to Northern Ferrax; and during a festival, incapacitated a large portion of the cities watch by lacing great barrels of ale with sleeping powder. 

After this small band of Galivanti lowered the drawbridge, and raised the portculus, they simply lit a series of three torches atop on of the bridge house towers; signaling to waiting Fontana Axebeak Cavalry nearby that the crossing was secured. 

In a single night, the cities weakened militia collapsed; and by mid-day the next morning, the cities Signoria capitulated; signing over their charter to Fontana, in exchange for limited administrative autonomy. 

---

Now with the Civil War at a standstill, and a tentative peace holding over Ferrax, Gavaria slowly simmers like a kettlepot; with protests, worker strokes, and minor acts of insurrection flaring from its lower wards.

[[House Fontana]] has had to dedicate a sizable portion of its forces to garrison the city, yet despite the unrest, Fontana has transformed Gavaria into its new seat of power; expanding its river shipyards, and refortifying old bastions.
