# Kobellstone

Seen from above with Griffin eye view, far up in the clouds

At the very heart of [[Ferrax]] lies the seat of power of the [[House Kobielli|Avralius]]. A Colossal sprawl of stone and marble that stretches for as far as the eye can see. Those who have seen it with a Griffin's eye view say that it gleams like a shining pearl, surrounded by waves of gold wheat and barley from horizon to horizon. 

Kobellstone itself is comprised of three cities, : Kobellstone, Vivarium, and Old Avralius. Each connected by intricate networks of well maintained imperial roads; and each a fortress unto itself. Yet over the centuries, as each city began to sprawl out with its erupting population, the distinction between cities disappeared, until they had essentially fused together in a massive labyrinthine like metropolis.

# Relations

### [[House Kobielli|Kobielli]] and [[House Fontana|Fontana]]


[[House Fontana]] remain a client vassal loyal to Avralius, bound to them by marriage and blood through younger branches of the family; with [[Baro Giotto de Fontain]] married to a cousin of [[Buto Kobielli]], cementing Fontana's loyalty to Avralius's central authority. 

Yet the alliance is not without its strain; as many [[House Kobielli|Kobielli]] see Fontana as their lesser, a house that is too desparate... To mercantile. But their sealing bond is one of utility; and they are too valuable to dismiss, yet too new to ever be seen as an equal. 

House Fontana owes its very existence to the patronage of the [[House Kobielli|Avralius]], and know that they are both Servant and Symbol; a for all to see that the [[House Kobielli|Avralius]] reward merit. And as such; they see [[House Kobielli]] as both Father and Keeper; for they are the patron whose blessing sustains them, yet whose shadow they cannot escape.

### [[House Kobielli|Kobielli]] and [[High Reach]]

Unlike the relationship of convenience and vassalage that the [[House Kobielli|Kobielli]] share with  [[House Fontana]], the Kobielli view [[High Reach]] as equals; with a centuries old pact formed by blood. Princeps [[Buto Kobielli]] himself, a halfling of the ancient Avralian line, is married to [[Vera Dell Valli]]; the sister of [[Rork Della Valli]], High Lord of [[High Reach]].

This union renewed an oath sworn generations ago between the mountain houses of Della Valli, and the [[House Kobielli|Avralius]]; in exchange for the [[High Reach]]'s defense, and precious iron; the [[House Kobielli|Avralius]] supply the Dwarves and Goliaths of the mountains with an abundance of wheat, barley, meats, and ale; as well as full autonomy in internal governance.


Where the Fontains stand to serve [[House Kobielli|Avralius]] proudly, [[High Reach]] stands besides them. 



### [[House Kobielli|Kobielli]] and [[Rentz]]

[[House Kobielli]] view the new upstart [[Rentz]] as a famly of wealth and influence, skilled in manipulating debts and trade across Ferrax. The Kobielli see the [[Rentz]] as a useful tool, rather than equals; and their influence is ackknowledged, but always carefully monitored. 

For the Kobielli, the [[Rentz]]'s slow and methodical infiltration into noble bloodlines, and subtle manipulation of the Fontain family is tolerated; so long as it serves the interests of the [[House Kobielli|Avralius]]. As such, [[House Kobielli]] extends power indirectly through the [[Rentz]] financial networks; and the banking family is well aware that if they lose the patronage of [[House Kobielli]], the careful plans they have laid will come crashing down around them.

# Legacy of Conquest


Before the Empire dared to cross the Laurel Strait, the fertile plains and river vales of Ferrax were a tapestry of native clans, and the occasional Imperial trade post. Of these, the largest clan holding of Avralius dominated the fertile steppe of central. 

Controlling the richest lands, the small Halfling dominated clans spread rapidly; establishing major trade routes between various settlements of the ancient land. Their influence made them a natural point of contact with [[Aleria]].

After Years of planning, and set up, when the Alerian Empire's Legions set foot on Ferrax, they did so with speed and precision; with iron sword, and silver pressing hands. Using the carefully laid out network of [[House Kobielli|Avrialian]] roads, the Legions swept over the untamed steppes of [[Ferrax]], bringing the entire continent to heel in less than a decade. 


[[Decimus Kobiellus]] was a rising star in [[Allerian]] politics; a Military Tribune during the conquest of Ferrax, he sought to not merely subdue the natives, but to integrate them into the Empire; and leading by example, he secured a marriage with the [[House Kobielli|Avralius]]'s chiefs daughter; bringing the clans firmly, and bloodleslly, into the the Empire's control. 

---

Over the centuries, the Kobielli expanded their influence across the central steppe; solidifying their dominance over the region through marriage, patronage, and the occasional skirmish. They bound minor families to them, and create loyal vassals through service. To this day, these bonds create a network of loyalty and obligation that extends across hundreds of leagues.

# The Ferraxian Civil War - The Kobielli

The Kobielli, while the dominant military faction on the continent, had to defend against Bandits from the North; as Fort Laurel fell quickly to a renegade garrison, The Talsberg to the North West, though little more than border skirmishes occurred, and the [[Delrath]]th to the south; who had pressed an early advantage against the Fontana and conquered a significant portion of land. By the end of the war, the Barbalani still held much of the land they had taken from the Fontana, though the united Avralius faction had taken Castellum Rosso, and half of the river city of Pontium to the South-East.


When the Unweaving took [[Aleria]], Ferrax became one of the last bastions of Imperial authority; and [[House Kobielli]], as the oldest and most powerful Imperial House, assumed the Mantle of Responsibility. [[Kobellstone]] served as a fortified hub of Governance for the Kobielli for centuries, yet even the [[Assembly of Houses]] was shaken by the devastation of their homeland, and many nobles that were not outright aligned with [[House Kobielli]] abandoned the city for their own seats of power.

### The Breaking

In the early months, bandits and raiders took advantage of the chaos; with the garrison of [[Fort Laurel]]  falling to a renegade garrison.

[[The Talsberg]] saw a chance to preserve their independence; and sent many of their war bands to the their shared border with [[House Kobielli]]; leading to the occasional deadly skirmish, and forcing [[Buto Kobielli]] to garrison important troops on the border, and within [[Kobellstone]]. 

It was to the South, however, that [[Buto Kobielli]] had his eyes afixed. For the ruthless [[Hiltegundis Barbalani]] pressed early advantages in the chaos of the Unweaving; seizing large swaths of fertile land from [[House Fontana]] on the northern banks of the Pontus River. 

### The Slow March

Yet the [[House Kobielli|Kobielli]] response was methodical, if not a little ponderous and slow. They mobilized the unified Avrialian armies of [[High Reach]] and select elite contingents of [[House Fontana]] Axe Beak Cavalry. 

Through a series of pitched battles, the [[House Kobielli|Avrialian]] union was able to seize control of [[Castellum Ross]] from the Delrath; a vital fortress city that lay halfway between [[Kobellstone]] and the city of [[Delrath]] itself. 


### The Battle of Pontium

While the war raged on the north bank of the Pontus River, the city of Pontium became the war's most bitter prize.

Straddling the Pontus River, Pontium (I'm very original) had always been a symbol of unity; a city of bridges, and trade; the city itself laying at the Southern most mouth of the river, and spilling into the seas beyond. 

Avralius forces, supported by shock infantry from the [[Montagni]], laid siege to the northern districts of the city for three long months; with little to no progress. 

In a brazen mid-day assault, a small party of mercenaries set off several large explosive barrels at key points within the city; including two of the three bridges that connected the city; before making their escape on a stolen river galley. 

This assault signaled the beginning of a two pronged attack; as [[House Kobielli|Kobielli]] Griffin Knights landed at near the cities main Forum, and the remaining Bridge; while a joint Fontana-Montagni northern assault quickly overwhelmed the confused city garrison.

Despite their initial success, The Battle of Pontium devolved into a stalemate; with the northern half of the city firmly in Avralius control, and the other firmly held by the [[Delrath]]th. For the remainder of the Civil War, open warfare in Pontium devolved into bitter river crossings, and small bands committing acts of assassination sabotage. 

---


By the conclusion of the war, [[House Kobielli]] had successfully defended the heartland of [[Ferrax]], and maintained unity between the [[House Kobielli|Avrialian]] vassals; and while [[Delrath]] still holds significant southern territories, and bandit uprising continue in the north, Kobielli has asserted itself as the strongest contender to wear the fallen crown of [[Aleria]]; with both [[The Talsberg]] and [[Delrath]] reluctantly attend [[Assembly of Houses]] once more, in an uneasy unity.
