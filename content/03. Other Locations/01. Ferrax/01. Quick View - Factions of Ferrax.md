

# Major Factions & Hosues

## Avralius

[[House Kobielli]]
- Led by [[Buto Kobielli]].
- Ancient Noble House
- Powerful, ambitious, seeking former glory.
[[House Fontana]]
- Led by [[Giotto de Fontain]].
- Own land near River.
- New Nobility
- Prove themselves as rivals.
[[Rentz]]
- Led by [[Baroness Selene Rentz]]
- Banking family
- Buying debts from merchants and nobles
- In for the profit, and want to work in shadow.

## [[High Reach]]

[[Della Valli]]
- Led By [[Rork Della Valli]]
- Steadfast, Isolationists; but highly loyal to the Empire		
- Strength through Power. Answer the Empires Call! 		


[[Montagni]]
- Led By [[Reina Della Valli Montagni]]
- Explorative, expansionist. 		
- Loyal to Exploration & Riches		
- At odds with Della Valli on isloationism.


[[Kriegemirr Signoria ]]
- Merchant Marine Traders & Profiteers		
- "Mixed" Blood, but good terms with rest of High Reach		
- Totally not aligned with the Fiorellia 		




## Delrath

[[House Barbalani]]
- Led by [[Hiltegundis Barbalani]]
- Power thirsty, bloody thirsty, and not hiding it.		
- Large Patrician House, large merchant navy		

[[House Aangrlio]]
- Led by [[Anneke de Delrotti]]
- For the People. For the Empire!		
- Old, honored name. weakended state. 		

[[Hans Matelizi ]]
- Owns small farming villages
- Visions of grandeur, willing to overspend and over tax to buy mercenaries.
- Currently holding multiple Mercenary contracts on standby during Peacetime.

## Talsberg

[[House Talio]]
-  Led by [[Cecco de la Wald]].
- Natives of Ferrix, old blood, isolationist & separtist.		
- Attempting to secure their borders despite cease fire		

[[House Boscaioli]]
- Natives of Ferrix & original Legions. 		
- Not "Pure Blooded" Ferrixian, but 'good enough.'		
- Must Prove Their Worth to Ferrix, always.		

[[Fiorellia ]]
- Led By [[Catalina Fiorellia ]]
- Merchant Marine, "New" Blood, considered outsiders.		
- At odds with Talio, but tentative allies.		
- May or may not support piracy. 		
- Totally not aligned with Kriegemirr 		


## Gavaria
- "Island" City State.		
- Recently 'Aquired' by [[House Fontana]]
- May, or may not be attempted to regain sovereignty. 		


## Villius/Ulfhednar.

- Led By Thegn [[Authketill Brodd]]
- Frontier State, lumber & fur traders.		
- Actively raiding into [[Fae Barbaria]] (And also [[Delrath]])		
- Generally Autonomous, as long as tithes & taxes are paid.		
- More closely related to Barbarians than Empire, but still loyal.
