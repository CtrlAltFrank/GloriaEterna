***Drums.*** [[Yonas]] uses her Drums as a spellcasting focus for her bard spells.

### Actions
***Shortsword.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage plus 21 (6d6) psychic damage. Yonas's attacks are magically toxic to a creature's mind.
:
***Song of Terror (1/Short or Long Rest).*** [[Yonas]] plays her Drums to seed paranoia and fear into the minds of 2 creatures of her choice within 60 feet, who must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened of [[Yonas]] or another creature of her choice for 1 round.
:
***Mantle of Whispers: Use Shadow.*** [[Yonas]] consumes the shadow of a dead humanoid acquired with her Mantle of Whispers: Capture Shadow, taking the form of the humanoid. While in disguise, she gains access to all information that the humanoid would freely share with a casual acquaintance, including general details of its background and personal life but not secrets. Another creature can see through this disguise by succeeding on a Wisdom (Insight) check contested by [[Yonas]]'s Charisma (Deception) check. [[Yonas]] gains a +5 bonus to her check.
:
***Spellcasting.*** [[Yonas]] casts one of the following spells, using Charisma as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 16, +8 to hit with spell attacks):
:
At will: :: *mending*, *blade ward*, *prestidigitation*
4/day each: :: *earth tremor (ee)*, *unseen servant*, *charm person*
3/day each: :: *greater invisibility*, *hallucinatory terrain*, *confusion*, *stinking cloud*, *dispel magic*, *locate animals or plants*, *suggestion*
2/day each: :: *modify memory*, *dominate person*, *legend lore*
1/day each: :: *mass suggestion*
### Bonus Actions
***Bardic Inspiration (5/Short or Long Rest).*** Through stirring words or playing her Drums, [[Yonas]] inspires a creature within 60 feet of her who can hear her. Once within the next 10 minutes, the creature can roll a d10 and add the number rolled to one ability check, attack roll, or saving throw it makes.

### Reactions
***Mantle of Whispers: Capture Shadow (1/Short or Long Rest).*** When a humanoid dies within 30 feet of [[Yonas]], she can magically capture its shadow.
