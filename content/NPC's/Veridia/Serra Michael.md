**Name**: Serra Michael
**Race**: High Elf
**Age**: 57
**Rank/Profession:** Ritual Singer & Storyteller / Actor
**Faction**: [[4. Civilians|Veridian Civilian]]
**General Appearance:** 
- Tall and Slender. Voice is ethereal, yet cracks. Their outfit is covered in black soot.
- They carry a polished wooden loot, yet do not play very well.
**Personality**:
- Awkward, a little hy, and nostalgic. He comforts children and teens with song and laments the loss of art and ceremony.
**Hooks**: 
- They seek performers to help stage a remembrance play for the dead. 
- Attempts to coax the players into paying for a 'restful song'. 
**Backstory**:
- Serra was a director of one of Veridia's great amphitheaters. After the Unweaving silenced the crowds, he carried his lyre to Aurea, offering song and sotry to preserve the memories of the lost world.
