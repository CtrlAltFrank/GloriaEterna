**Name**: Verisha
**Race**: HUman
**Age**: 19
**Rank/Profession:** Student, Scribe Apprentice 
**Faction**: [[4. Civilians|Veridian Civilian]]
**General Appearance:** 
- Thin and wide eyed. Her hands are stained in ink. She wears dirty robes that were once gray, now stained with dirt, ink, and blood. 
- She is always clutching a battered, cracked writing slate. 
**Personality**:
- She is inquisitive, albeit shy. She will scribble down everything she sees that is new, or attempt to, as ink is running low.
**Hooks**: 
- Offers to copy or attempt to translate any texts the players bring her. 
- Her ink stores are almost out, and she does not have a method to replace them aside from using her own blood, or charcoal. 
**Backstory**:
Verisha was training under [[Domus Aurea]]'s archivist when the Unweaving struck; and while they were out for the night, she was confined to the study copying message scrolls that were to be sent out in the morning.
- She now works piecemeal, copying petitions, and ledger entries for labor tasks.
