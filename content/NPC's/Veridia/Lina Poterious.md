**Name**: Lina Poterious
**Race**: Human
**Age**: 33
**Rank/Profession:** Former Shopkeep, now Barter Coordinator 
**Faction**: [[4. Civilians|Veridian Civilian]]
**General Appearance:** 
- Patched, yet tidy tunic. Simple necklace.
**Personality**:
- Willing to Haggle.
- Everything has a price, it just depends if it is an affordable price.
**Hooks**: 
- Offers discounted supplies if someone is willing to help find her old pre-Unweaving caravan. 
- Worries that the black-market traders are undercutting her prices.
- Believes that those same black-market traders are Loyalist spies.
- Still searching for her missing niece. 
**Backstory**:
- Lina ran a fine cloth and spice stall in the Silk Market. The Unweaving destroyed her livelihood, and now she helps coordinate trades between artisans, guards, and refugees to keep the economy alive.
