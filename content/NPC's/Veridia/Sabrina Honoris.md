**Name**: Sabrina Honoris
**Race**: Human
**Age**: 52
**Rank/Profession:** High Priestess of Honus
**Faction**: [[3. The Faithful|The Faithful]]
**General Appearance:**
- Sturdy and statuesque. Draped in crimson and gold robes.
- Multiple scars on her arms and chest.
- Has a sickle and dried grain strapped to her hips.
**Personality**:
- Ruthless devotion, with maternal care.
- Life and death are inseparable.
**Hooks**: 
- She demands ritual sacrifice to atone for the failed harvests inside the mountain.
- There is a rumor that she orchestrated a plague to cull the weak.
- She believes that bloodletting will appease Honus in these dire times.
**Backstory**:
- Originally from Ferrax, she moved to Veridia and became a celebrated herbalist.
- Her faith was hardened when the Unweaving claimed her twin daughters; she ensures that every seed grows, and every life is reaped on schedule.
