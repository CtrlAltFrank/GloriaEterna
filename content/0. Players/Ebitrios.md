- He does truly believe that he is a tool of Honus

# Backstory:
What can a man do when that which makes him ceases to be? Does he admit to the public his now inability to do what he previously could? Or does he take matters into his own hands? In the absence of a god…is man made to act as one?
A question for the most unfortunate among us, but a question that had to be answered by one Duke Roland Auris Fantana after the goddess of fertility, Honus, seemingly turned her back on him and his family. After years and years of attempts to reconnect with his once claim to fame, The Auris Fantana house found themselves in hot water after their eldest and most rebellious daughter, Noble-lady Willow Auris Fantana, began spreading rumors that Honus had excommunicated her family. Her father needed to act quickly as public opinion began to turn against his house. Duke Roland used his connections with practitioners of the dark arts to end all doubt against his family with an act that directly goes against Honus' teachings.
A skeleton crafted of gold lay bare atop a glyph-carved table. Ancient texts that read of alchemy and artificial men were stacked in each corner of the room. Duke Roland and Duchess Venus gritted their teeth as serrated blades sliced deeply into the tissue of their palms. The deep red ichor that emerged from their hands met in the center of the bare human's skeletal frame. The crimson sap trailed upwards towards a large, dull gem that was placed in a golden collar around the skeleton's throat, fusing with it until it began to emit an eerie red glow.
Flesh, muscle, and all sorts of undulating viscera began to erupt from the sides of the gem. The organic tissue wrapped itself around the appendages of the human-like frame in a violent yet intentional manner. The mass of meat crawled its way toward the embedded braziers that were attached to the back of the newly forming being's hands. The motion was only stopped when two smaller gems were implanted within the brazier's slots. The body had been made whole. The once barren metallic frame was now made human. An androgyous yet beautiful being of porcelain pure white skin now lies before them. After minutes of anticipation, the being came to life with the sounds of hollow, strained coughs.
Ebitrios would be his name, and from this point forward, he would be known as the youngest son of the Auris Fantana family. Though starting his life in a fully grown human’s body, he was immediately adept at using the hidden gems within his mortal being to deceive his family's detractors into believing that Honus had stepped away from the family for just a moment, so her to create and gift them with the perfect child. A child who was blessed with the natural abilities of a god.
It took 14 years for Ebitrios to fully understand how to be human, and another 10 to learn the ways of a noble. But despite it all, Ebitrios became a centerpiece of the family.
This inkling of doubt grew inside of her and eventually led Willow to take drastic actions against her youngest sibling. During a public welcome religious observance at the Auris Fantana estate, an assassination attempt was levied at Ebitrios. Though he survived and the assassin was apprehended, the gem within his golden collar was partially cracked. Ebitrios continued as planned, but now he seems to be plagued with periodic coughing fits.
Despite it all, the youngest Auris Fantana elected himself as the one to assist in the wars raging across his homeland, as he saw himself as a tool of Honus herself.
Backstory Summary:
Born of desperation and alchemy, Ebitrios was created by Duke Roland Auris Fantana to restore his family's honor after their goddess, Honus, seemingly forsook them—his divine appearance deceived the public into believing he was a miracle, not a fabrication. Though accepted as the perfect child and rising to nobility, a cracked gem from a failed assassination left him plagued and uncertain, yet he fights in the name of the goddess who never made him.
