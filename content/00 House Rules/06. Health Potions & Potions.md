

Gods I hate how lame health potions. 


### HP Regained
- Instead of the normal d4, Health Potions are d6 based

### Health Potion Actions
- Using a BONUS Action to drink a potion allows for the standard role (ex. 2d6+2)
- Using a NORMAL Action to drink a potion will take the full effect of the potion. 

### CRAFTING

- Ability to Cast **"Cure Wounds"**, or a **Scroll of Cure Wounds**
- **Spell Slot** ^1
- Herbalism Kit AND Alchemists Supplies or Brewing Kit
- Long Rest or equivalent downtime / Reasonably safe space
- A proper container.
- ~~Materials~~ ^2

1: The Spell Slot can either be a left over unused spell slot, or subtracted from the Long Rest; this rule is subject to change.

2: Materials may be required; but that is more to add more roleplay/interaction elements, and will be discussed if someone wishes to craft.
