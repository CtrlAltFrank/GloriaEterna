
Taken from Good Ol' 4e.


Mostly a narrative effect, but when a Mod/Monster/NPC hits 50% HP, they gain the Bloodied Condition. 

- A Bloodied Creature may recharge a Skill automatically. 
- A Bloodied Creature may attempt to flee.
- A Bloodied Creature may release spores, gas, etc when damaged enough.
- A Bloodied Creature may lose its ability to fly.
Etc etc.
