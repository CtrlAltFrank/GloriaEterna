## The Fall of Gloria Eternia: Primer

In the 73rd year of the [[Age of Light]], the Eternal Empire, Gloria Eternia, stood at the pinnacle of civilization. At its heart, the continent of [[Aleria]], a vibrant land home to over a hundred million citizens who knew little of want or need; as Grand coliseums and amphitheaters graced every city, and great forums and Bazaars thrived, fed by the constant flow of luxury goods and vital commodities from the vast colonies of Gloria [[Ferrax]] and Gloria Keldoran. New magics were formed and weaved in vast Collegea and Universities, open to all citizens who showed even a modicum of skill. All the whole, the Empires Legions pushed ever onwards, expanding into foreign and treacherous [[Fae Barbaria]] 

  

In a single night, the light of Gloria Eternia was snuffed out. As the weave of magic across the entire world came unwound. [[Aleria]], the very soul of the Empire, was desolated in the Unweaving. The land itself became a corrupted wasteland, its once-thriving natural splendor, teeming with life twisted into grotesque parodies; its populace, transformed into the gibbering Shriven, mere corrupted shades of their former selves. 

  

In the aftermath, civil strife gripped Gloria [[Ferrax]], the largest surviving colony. Now, that struggle has ended, and Gloria [[Ferrax]] stands as the Empire's new, albeit uneasy, center. Though still struggling to hold back the wildlands of [[Fae Barbaria]] to the west and the encroaching sands of the Southern Wastes, the new [[Assembly of Nobility]] looks homeward once more, towards Mortuus [[Aleria]]. Their gaze fixed upon reclaiming their ancestral homeland, driving back the corruption, and recovering the lost magics and untold treasures entombed within its shattered ruins. The age of unchallenged prosperity is over, and now in this, the 4th year of the [[Age of Shadow]], the reconquest has begun.

## The Last Plea

The news arrived on the tide. No with the fanfare of a returning hero that it deserved, but with the quiet desperation of the dying. 

  

Seven years after the Unweaving tore [[Aleria]] asunder, a small fishing boat, little more than a skiff; its boards splintered, and its sails ragged and torn, was spotted off the coast of the harbor at [[Kriegmirr]].



And yet, a single emaciated figure clung to this half sunken wreck. Their uniform, though torn and stained with mud and blood, bore the markings of an Imperial Alerian messenger. They collapsed onto the wooden frame of the docks moments after being pulled from their wrecked vessel. Their breath ragged and broken, eyes wide and sunken. Clutched in their lifeless hands, and tucked within their sea sodden cloak, were the signet rings of two prominent houses of [[Aleria]], bound tightly in chains around a small leather bag. The contents of which held the last desperate pleas of a lone besieged city, that against all odds, held true against the Unweaving, and the desolation of the past seven years. 

  

After seven long and silent years, the City of[[Veridia]] still stood. The beleaguered populace has been driven underground, seeking refuge from the [[Shades]] and [[Shriven]] above. And yet The [[Corruption]] of the [[Unweaving]] has begun to once more press their defenses to breaking. The magics that protected this once proud city are faltering, and supplies once abundant, now run low.

  

With [[Shades]] and [[Shriven]] now entering the tunnels where the last desperate few still yet hold the gates, a final cry for salvation from the horrors of the [[Unweaving]] echoes out.
