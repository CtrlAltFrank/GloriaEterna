

### Help Action

You can assist with a skill check if you are proficient with the same skill. 
You can assist with a skill check with another Skill, if it fits the narrative.

Instead of Advantage or the like, you give the person you are helping a 1d8 roll in addition to their roll. 

The Helper rolls the d8. 


### Take 10

You can  declare that you wish to Take 10 on a Skill you are Proficient with. Meaning that you will take a little longer in game, but you will take an automatic 10+ your Modifier



### Group Checks 

Simple & Easy. If there is a Group Skill Check, you can use a skill you are Proficient, that has NOT yet been used by another player; if it fits in the narrative.
