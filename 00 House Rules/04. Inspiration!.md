

Shamelessly stolen from KIDS ON BIKES, and modified because I really like those coins I bought.


**Bronze** Represent common and small strokes of luck.
- Add +1 to any result (Attack, Damage, Skillcheck)
- Add a minor narrative declaration. (Character Remember's something, finds a common item, a falling player grabs a ledge just in time.)

**Silver**  Represent better luck, and skills gained. 
- Re-roll any Skill Check or Attack; must take the second roll. 
- Gain +5 to any Roll
- Spend an additional HD on a Short Result

**Gold** Represts possible Divine Favor,
- Automatic 20 on a Death Saving Throw
- Recharge/Regain a Spell Slot
- Gain +10 to Any Roll
- Add a Major Narrative declaration (A Bridge that the party is crossing begins to collapse, but will hold just long enough for the players to safely get across / A sudden downpour of rain hides the players approach.)

Can you convert these to other coins? Why yes you can. 5 Bronze = 1 Silver, 2 Silver = 1 Gold; and same in reverse.
