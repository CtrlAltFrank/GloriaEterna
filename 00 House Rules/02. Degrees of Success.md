-  If the Result is equal to or less than the DC by 10, it is a Critical Fumble
-  If the Result is equal to or greater than the DC by 10, it is a Critical Success 

Examples:
- ###### A player is climbing a cliff, with a DC 15...
   - ...and rolls a total of 25 (Nat 18+7 Modifiers); they climb the cliff, at no cost to movement. 
   - ...and rolls a total of 5 (Nat 2+3 Modifiers); they fall from the clifface, and land prone.


Why Not take damage? Because you didn't roll a nat 1, and double negative effects aren't fun.
