
# Gloria Eternia, Character Creation Rules.

https://www.dndbeyond.com/campaigns/join/68668211017879509

###### **Point Buy**
- Frankie’s Heroic Array: 16, 15, 14, 14, 12, 10


###### **Class Rules**
- Single Class or Dual Classing Only
 - No more than 2 classes, total.
- Multiclass Responsibly. 
 - I love cheese, but I’m lactose intolerant. 


###### **Hit Points**
- Take MAXIMUM Hit Points at every level.


###### **Feats & ASI (Ability score improvements)**
- Take a single Feat to start, with DM approval. 
- Take BOTH when a level up allows.

###### **Character Motivation Requirements**
- Your character must have a reason to adventure other than pure greed, lust for power, or because they’re an outcast.

###### **Backstory**
- Minimum 2 sentences, maximum 1 Page.
 - I f you have lore questions, feel free to ask!
- Create 3 character Goals, keep them private from other players, but send them to the DM. 
 - 1 Long Term Goal, 2 Short Term Goals.
- Create 3 “Rumors” about your character, that other players may have heard about. 
 - All 3 can be true, false, or any combination. Just let me know what is what.
- Characters should be capable of working with the party. No solo adventuring every session. 
- Don’t try and solo the plot, work as a team.
- Don’t try and betray the party at every opportunity
- *If you have a cringy monologue, I may give you inspiration, but I may also judge you. Possibly both.*

### **Race / Species Restrictions & Limitations**

###### **Restricted**:
 - Aarakocra
 - Bugbear
 - Centaur
 - Dragonborn
 - Gith
 - Goblin
 - Hobgoblin
 - Kenku
 - Kobold
 - Lizardfolk
 - Tabaxi
 - Tortle
 - Triton
 - Warforged
 - Yuan-ti
 - Gnomes (see below)

###### **Limitations**
 - Wood Elves,
 - Monstrous Creatures (Cyclops, Minotaur, Satyr, etc).
You can choose one of these as a race, but be advised, there will be Roleplay & Lore consequence. 

###### **Lore Reasons: **
- Fae Barbaria, Enemies of the Empire: Bugbear, Goblinoids, Tabaxi, Centaurs.
 - These are known tribal raiders, bandits, beastkin, and savages of the wilds.


- Jade Empire, Enemies of the Empire: Lizardfolk, Kenku, Kobold, Dragonborn
 - The Jade Empire once struck a near mortal blow to the Empire, only a few decades ago. These races are still seen as enemies of the Empire. 


**GNOMES & KENDER: **
        I hate these insufferable, smug little tinker-demons. If you can PUNT it, it’s not a real person. They are PESTS at best. 

**Tortles:**
Please see “ Why the Giant Tortoise did not receive a scientific name for 300 years after its discovery”

###### **Additional Compendiums:**
- Third-Party Material IS allowed with DM Approval
 - Just don’t super duper powergame it.

###### **Items & Gear**
- Starting Gear is FAIR GAME
 - Additionally, if you can justify mundane tools, supplies, belongings, etc, go for it. As long as you can actually *carry* it all. 
- Begin with ONE (1) Magical / Wondrous Item, at +1 Bonus Max.
- Pending DM Approval.

Be Cool, Be Creative, Be Cooperative.
Don’t be a Dingus
Say NO to Powergaming, YES to Minmaxing Roleplaying
